One valley. Three centuries. All of them running.

1890 has iron ore a shovel deep, a river, a forest and no electricity of any kind. 2037 has a national grid, steel and a caster — standing on an ore body four generations already went through. 2070 has the two best processes on the map and nothing whatever to put in one. None of them can finish alone.

What is different

Nothing is researched and nothing is locked. A motor in 1890 is a price — 1,600 gears somebody in 2037 has to make and ship backwards — through a fracture that only stays open while somebody's grid is holding it. Material remembers which century it came out of, so ore carried through 2037 untouched is still 1890 ore, and ore crushed there is not.

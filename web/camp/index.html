Five factories, one clock, and none of them stop.

Coal Basin has more fuel than it can burn on a platform too small for the plant it needs. Iron Valley has all the land in the world and no fuel. Manufacturing has neither, and a minute of railway between it and both. Finish one and it starts supplying the others — and keeps doing it while you are somewhere else.

Join the campaign

What is different

Twelve components unlock as rooms come online — a separator, a press, a condenser — never a percentage. Machines you design go on a shelf and can be copied into the next room and changed there. Trains between rooms are real, take real time, and arrive whether anybody is watching or not.
